## Sending rules

1. **Send between 10 AM – 12 PM or 4 PM – 7 PM.** Shop owners actually read their phone then.
2. **Never send on Friday before 2 PM.**
3. **Always address them by shop name**, not "Sir/Madam". Copy-paste feels obvious otherwise.
4. **One message = one CTA.** Don't stack questions.
5. **Send max 30–40/day.** WhatsApp flags bulk sending — spread it out, personalize each.

## Reply-rate boosters

- Attach **one photo** of the shop's own Google Maps listing (shows you did homework).
- End with a time-bound question: *"Can I drop by tomorrow 11 AM?"* — easier to reply yes/no than open-ended.
