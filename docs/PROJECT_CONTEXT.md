# Rezzy Project Context

## Product
Rezzy is a booking SaaS platform.

Primary customers:
- salons
- spas
- barbershops
- beauty businesses
- clinics
- home services

---

# Main Goal
Build an AI-powered business operating system around Rezzy.

---

# Founder Constraints
- Solo founder
- Working full-time job
- Limited operational bandwidth
- Needs high automation

---

# Current Priority
Reduce manual business operations using AI.

---

# Current Focus
Phase 1 — AI Receptionist

---

# Long-Term Vision
Rezzy should eventually provide:
- AI receptionist
- AI lead management
- AI reminders
- AI customer support
- AI marketing tools

for business owners.

---

# Core Principles
- Build phase-by-phase
- Human verification before scaling
- Automation first
- Keep systems modular
- Avoid overengineering early
