## Visual direction

- **Colors:** 2-color palette max. Brand color + white. Avoid loud gradients.
- **Font:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, Poppins, Montserrat). Arabic: Cairo or Tajawal.
- **Imagery:** Real Sharjah shop photos (ask Founding Shops for permission) > stock photos. Always.
- **Tool:** Canva free tier. Use "Instagram Carousel" template.

---

## Posting rules

1. **Post Sun–Wed, 7–9 PM Sharjah time.** Peak UAE SME engagement.
2. **Reply to every comment within 1 hour** for the first 3 hours after posting. Algorithm boost.
3. **Pin the carousel to your profile** — it's your pitch deck.
4. **Cross-post the reel to TikTok** with the same caption.
5. **One CTA per post.** Don't mix "DM us" + "visit website" + "comment" — pick one.
