# REELS GENERATION RULES

When generating reels:
- Hook must be within first 2 seconds
- Keep content highly visual
- Use short punchy sentences
- Focus on relatable business pain
- Include CTA at end

Reels should include:
1. Hook
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. CTA

Example CTA:
- "DM us 'BOOKING' to automate your salon"
- "Book your free demo today"
- "Stop losing clients manually"
